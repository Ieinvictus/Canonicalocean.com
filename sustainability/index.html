OUR APPROACH

REFRESHMENT WITH RESPONSIBILITY.

At Canonical Ocean, sustainability is part of the way we think about water and beverages. We aim to make thoughtful decisions across sourcing, packaging, production and distribution.

Our goal is simple — deliver refreshing products while continuously looking for better ways to reduce waste, use resources responsibly and protect the environments connected to water.

01

PROTECT THE SOURCE

Water begins with nature. We believe responsible beverage production starts with respecting the environments and water resources connected to every product we create.

02

PACKAGING WITH PURPOSE

Packaging protects the product from source to consumer. We are focused on responsible packaging choices, efficient material use and opportunities to improve recyclability across our beverage range.

WATER STEWARDSHIP

EVERY DROP MATTERS.

Water is at the heart of Canonical Ocean. Responsible water stewardship means understanding the importance of water resources and continuously working toward efficient and thoughtful use.

We support a culture of conservation — from responsible operations to greater awareness of water protection throughout our value chain.
